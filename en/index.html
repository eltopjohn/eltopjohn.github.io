Good to know

How many people can play?
2 to 8, on one phone that gets passed around. Nobody else needs to install anything.
Does it need internet?
No. The cards live inside the app, so it plays offline.
Is it free?
It is free to download and comes with 40 cards. A single in-app purchase unlocks all 130. There is no subscription.
Any ads or sign-up?
None. No ads, no account, no permissions.
Which languages?
English and Spanish. All 130 cards come in both, and you switch language from the menu.
What are the questions about?
11 categories: music, geography, brands, symbols, numbers, literature, history, science, nature, film & TV, and sport. Some cards use photos or sound.
How long is a game?
As long as the table wants: play to a score (10, 15, 20 or 25) or to a number of cards (5, 10, 15 or 20), and stop whenever you like.
What does it run on?
iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets.
